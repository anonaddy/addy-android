What's new in addy.io for Android 6.6.0
Highlights
- Major under-the-hood rewrite of the codebase for better performance and stability
- Added pagination and loading indicators when scrolling through aliases, blocklist, and failed deliveries
- Long-pressing to select multiple aliases is now enabled by default
- Reordering rules is now smoother and only syncs when you let go
Fixed/Improved
- Improved widget scaling and theming across different launcher grid sizes
- Fixed UI padding and button clipping across various screens
- Performance improvements and other things to make the app even smoother
